# The Mathematics of Pricing and Discounts

## Project Overview

This beginner-level Python project explores how different discount rates affect revenue and profitability.

The analysis uses a simulated dataset containing three products: Printer, Toner, and Paper.

## Research Question

How do different discount rates affect revenue and profit?

## Objectives

- Calculate selling prices after applying discounts.
- Analyze total revenue under different discount scenarios.
- Calculate total profit and profit margins.
- Compare the financial impact of 0%, 10%, and 20% discounts.
- Visualize the results using Python.

## Mathematical Formulas

**Discounted Price**

\[
P_d = P(1-d)
\]

**Revenue**

\[
R = P_d \times Q
\]

**Profit**

\[
Profit = (P_d-C)\times Q
\]

**Profit Margin**

\[
Margin = \frac{Profit}{Revenue}\times 100
\]

## Key Results

| Discount | Total Revenue | Total Profit |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0% | €11,400 | €4,350 |
| 10% | €10,260 | €3,210 |
| 20% | €9,120 | €2,070 |

The results show that increasing discount rates reduces both revenue and profit when sales quantities remain constant.

## Limitations

The analysis assumes that the quantity sold remains constant regardless of the discount rate. In reality, discounts may influence customer demand and sales volume.
